# I 4

W: 2.2, which is good
W: 0.9, which is good
W: 3.0, which is fair

A: Changing surface moisture or temperature
W: The insulation is in perfect condition

W: The cable run is too short to test

Q: Why is IR alone not enough to accept a large medium-voltage cable?
A: It can miss local defects that only show under AC stress
W: It's too accurate and fails good cables too often
W: It damages XLPE cable at any test voltage used
W: It only measures the cable's length, not insulation

Q: A motor tests at 15 MΩ but PI is 3.0. Most likely:
A: Surface contamination; clean, dry and retest
W: A perfect motor; accept it as it stands
W: Shorted turns somewhere in one phase
W: An open winding on the motor's third phase
E: Good PI says the insulation body absorbs well. Low absolute value often comes from dirt and moisture.
